Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the voice-test application (the "App") handles your data.

1. Summary

The App does not collect your data. The App author does not operate servers for the App, and the App contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code. Your journal stays on your device.

2. Data that stays on your device

The App stores these items in its own storage on your device:

  • Audio recordings that you record in the App or import from Voice Memos, Files, the Share Sheet, or Shortcuts.
  • Transcripts, word timings, titles, and text entries.
  • Search data, embeddings, and topic groups that the App calculates from your entries.
  • Your App settings.

The App does all of this processing on your device. The bundled EmbeddingGemma model and Apple Foundation Models run on your device. The App does not send your entries to the App author or to any third party.

3. Speech recognition

The App uses Apple's speech recognition frameworks to transcribe audio. The main speech engine runs on your device. If the main engine cannot transcribe a recording, the App uses an older Apple speech engine, and iOS can send that audio to Apple for recognition. Apple processes that audio under the Apple Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The App author does not receive that audio or its transcript.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: the App uses the microphone only when you record a voice note.
  • Speech Recognition: the App uses speech recognition only to transcribe your voice notes.

You can turn off these permissions in the iOS Settings app at any time.

5. Backups and device sync

iOS can include App data in your iCloud or computer backups, according to your device settings. Apple controls those backups. The App author has no access to them.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete single entries in the App. You can delete all App data from the Settings screen in the App. When you delete the App, iOS deletes the App data from your device.

7. Children

The App does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

Later versions of the App can update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date shows the date of the most recent change.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: use the App Store support contact for this App.
