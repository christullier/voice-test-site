Common questions

Where is my data?

The app keeps your recordings, transcripts, and settings on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I import a recording?

In Voice Memos, open a recording, tap Share, and choose the app. You can also import with Shortcuts or from the Files app.

A transcript does not appear.

Keep the app open until the transcription finishes. Make sure that Speech Recognition is on in Settings > Privacy & Security.

How do I delete all data?

Open Settings in the app and use Clear Data, or delete the app.
